## Game assets

zubspace is a fan remake of SubSpace and Subspace Continuum. To look and play
like the original, it includes the original game's graphics, sounds, fonts and
maps, along with zones, maps and artwork made by the player community over the
years. All of it lives under `assets/`, except `js/zubspace_ships.png`,
`js/zubspace_icons.png` and the link-preview image `js/zubspace_share.png`,
which are made from the original sprites.

These assets belong to their original creators. They are not ours, they are not
covered by the MIT license, and we don't grant any rights to them. If you made
or own any of this material and want it credited or removed, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/pkieltyka/zubspace/issues).

Welcome Steam players!

Chaos/Pro League zone is the longest running zone in Subspace!
Based on the original SVS settings, it values skill over luck,
and the veteran players are the best in Subspace!

To ask for help - just type ?help <my question>

Basic play: 
arrows to move; ctrl for bullets; tab for bombs;
shift-ctrl to repel enemies and weapons.
watch your energy! When it goes to zero, you die!

Have fun!
